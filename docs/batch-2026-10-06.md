## Files in each memory

- `persona.md`: investor-specific preferences and diligence questions, distinguishing direct statements from inference.
- `theses.md`: investment themes, tensions and changes supported by the sources.
- `portfolio_and_constraints.md`: company/fund relationships, mandates and allocation constraints, with firm and personal roles distinguished.
- `evidence/` / `evidence.json`: quoted investor evidence mapped to the rationale taxonomy.
- `context.md` / `context.json`: cited role, portfolio and background facts.
- `sources.md`, `source_reviews.json`, `coverage.md` / `coverage.json`: source receipts, packet reviews and coverage gaps.
- `inventory.json`, `prepared.json`, `_manifest.json`, `BUILD_REPORT.md`: reproducibility and validation records.
