## Mandate and structural constraints

- RareBreed’s repeatedly stated mandate is private-company investment at pre-seed through seed, often as the first or near-first institutional check, primarily outside major U.S. technology hubs. [ev:mac-conwell-0315] [ev:mac-conwell-0670] [ctx:mac-conwell-0601] [ctx:mac-conwell-0602]

- The firm is broadly sector agnostic but explicitly excludes life sciences and therapeutics because Conwell says he cannot evaluate them competently. [ev:mac-conwell-0346] [ev:mac-conwell-0873]

- Preferred entry valuation was repeatedly below $10 million post-money, with selective exceptions; reported checks vary by source from $50,000–$250,000, while Conwell often described a $250,000 target. [ev:mac-conwell-0101] [ev:mac-conwell-0194] [ctx:mac-conwell-0552]

- RareBreed requires pro-rata rights, seeks information rights, and generally does not require a board seat; no supplied evidence establishes routine Conwell board service at portfolio companies. [ev:mac-conwell-0298]

- A stated portfolio rule limited one company to 5% of the fund or $500,000 across rounds; planned follow-ons numbered roughly six to ten, with emphasis on first checks and about 5% reserved for off-thesis opportunities. [ev:mac-conwell-0632] [ctx:mac-conwell-0513]

- Fund I was reported as a $10 million traditional vehicle with more than 200 LPs in one account and 194 investors in later, more precise testimony; 83.5% reportedly invested $50,000 or less. These counts likely reflect differing definitions or times rather than independent verification. [ctx:mac-conwell-1042] [ctx:mac-conwell-1043] [ctx:mac-conwell-0527]

- Conwell reported no personal GP commitment to Fund I because he could not afford one. This is a fund-structure fact, not evidence of weak alignment or a personal investment portfolio. [ev:mac-conwell-0287] [ctx:mac-conwell-0212]

- RareBreed’s post-investment model emphasizes follow-on fundraising, customer and partnership introductions, recruiting help, and direct founder access; assistance is most effective when founders communicate specific needs. [ev:mac-conwell-0627] [ev:mac-conwell-0628] [ctx:mac-conwell-0831]
