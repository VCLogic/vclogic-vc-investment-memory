## Mandate and constraints

- **General-purpose family office:** Hyatt describes exposure to public stocks, real estate, direct angels, PE, VC managers, funds of funds, acquisitions, and significant direct stakes. [ctx:michael-hyatt-0002] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0013] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0029]

- **Allocation is approximate and source-dependent:** A 2020 account used roughly one-third public markets, one-third real estate, and one-third PE including VC; CNBC’s 2022 report used the same thirds, while another account grouped stocks, PE/VC, and acquisitions or major stakes. [ctx:michael-hyatt-0477] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0092] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0050]

- **Reported return objective:** CNBC reported an 8% annual family-office goal in 2022; it should not be assumed current or applied to every asset class. [ctx:michael-hyatt-0092]

- **Private-company concentration:** Hyatt says larger checks into fewer companies, with active operating support, are his preferred direct-investing model. [ev:michael-hyatt-0069] [ev:michael-hyatt-0070]

- **No fund deployment clock:** He says he can wait ten years without another deal because his family office is not a conventional VC fund. [ev:michael-hyatt-0025] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0012]

- **Control preference:** He personally prefers larger or majority positions with governance power, though his record also includes minority angel investments and VC-led co-investments. [ev:michael-hyatt-0096] [ev:michael-hyatt-0097] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0017]

- **Fund-manager alignment:** CNBC reports a preference for funds whose founders have meaningful personal capital at risk; Hyatt also monitors fund statements and market quartiles. [ctx:michael-hyatt-0169] [ev:michael-hyatt-0114]

- **Liquidity and risk posture:** Public holdings were reported as largely passive ETF exposure with selected technology stocks, while the overall family-office objective emphasizes wealth preservation and willingness to miss exceptional opportunities. [ctx:michael-hyatt-0167] [ev:michael-hyatt-0196] [ev:michael-hyatt-0203]
