[ctx:rohit-gupta-future-communities-capital-0044] [ctx:rohit-gupta-future-communities-capital-0030]

- **Sector mandate:** The recurring core is technology modernizing legacy industries, particularly government, healthcare, finance, real estate/property technology, transportation, supply chain, climate, and energy.
