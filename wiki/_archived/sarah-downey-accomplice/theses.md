# Sarah Downey — Recurring Investment Theses

- **Direct early-stage venture — people and market dominate.** Explicitly, the two core angel criteria are the founding people and market opportunity; because evidence is scarce at pre-seed, founder quality often carries the decision. Exceptional founders can override many normal pass reasons, but the exception bar is extremely high.
