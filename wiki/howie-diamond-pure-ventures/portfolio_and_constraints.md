## Mandate and constraints

- **Stage:** Pure Ventures describes its mandate as early-stage, while Alpha Bridge’s 2018 strategy was more specifically post-seed with a clear Series A path. The latter should not be assumed to remain a current hard gate. [ctx:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0048] [ev:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0015]

- **Founder-development mandate:** Pure Ventures combines company investing with founder development, and Heartwood helps founders address people problems and transition into scaling leadership. [ctx:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0048] [ctx:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0049]

- **Sector scope:** Source reporting covers consumer/CPG, e-commerce, marketplaces, and frontier/deep-tech areas including biotech, AI, robotics, and aerospace; these are reported areas of focus, not evidenced quotas or exclusions. [ctx:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0003] [ctx:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0064]

- **Founder fit:** Ethical alignment and Diamond’s personal “Sunday test” are explicit constraints, while leadership, talent retention, grit, resilience, and execution are strong preferences. [ev:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0002] [ev:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0003] [ev:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0019] [ev:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0020]

- **Execution:** Ideas or speculation alone are insufficient in Diamond’s stated approach, but no universal revenue or product-market-fit threshold is disclosed. [ev:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0004]

- **Check size:** The only reliable specific range is The Pitch’s program-level $25,000–$30,000 typical check; it is not a Pure Ventures mandate. [ctx:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0035]

- **Terms:** The Teamable $30,000 SAFE at a $15 million post-money cap is a single disclosed deal and not evidence of a standard valuation ceiling or security preference. [ctx:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0040]

- **Support allocation:** Alpha Bridge allocated some fund resources to Project Atlas services for backed founders, demonstrating a historical willingness to fund portfolio support directly. [ctx:howie-diamond-pure-ventures-0068]

The record does not substantiate geography limits, fund size, ownership targets, reserves, lead/follow rules, board-seat policy, return hurdles, diversity quotas, LP exposure, family-office activity, majority acquisitions, public-market investing, or real-estate investing. These are unresolved limitations, not implied absences from Diamond’s actual activity.
