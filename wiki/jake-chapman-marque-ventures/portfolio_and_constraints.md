## Mandate and constraints

- Marque states that prospective investments must have a clear defense application and concern emerging capabilities for the United States and allies; another firm release extends the description to NATO and allied users. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0139] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0141] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0166]

- Marque reports investing from Seed through Series A and being willing to lead or follow. A third-party profile instead describes pre-seed through Series A, a $100,000–$5 million range, and a $1.5 million sweet spot; because the latter is undated aggregator data, it should not override the firm’s own mandate. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0140] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0179] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0197]

- The firm’s stated domain spans defense, manufacturing, and deep technology aligned with DoD and Intelligence Community needs; Chapman’s explicit interests include aerospace, space, drones, flight, AI, quantum, energy, synthetic biology, advanced manufacturing, and supporting technologies. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0094] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0079] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0080] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0119]

- Chapman explicitly rejects a dual-use-only constraint and has said he does not invest in purely consumer-facing, e-commerce, game-studio, or adtech businesses. The sector exclusion comes from an undated Signal profile identifying him with Alpha Bridge, so its chronology and application to Marque are unresolved. Alpha Bridge also described investing across deep tech and consumer, and The Pitch reports a consumer-health hardware investment. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0026] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0093] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0120] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0148]

- October 2023 material said Marque was raising $100 million for Fund I. That is a historical fundraising statement, not evidence of a final close, current assets under management, or present available capital. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0169]

- AVCC was described as a hybrid for-profit/nonprofit vehicle investing public and private dollars, while Alpha Bridge’s historical mandate involved seed or post-seed companies and founder development. Those mandates should not be conflated with Marque’s current fund. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0133] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0149] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0159]
