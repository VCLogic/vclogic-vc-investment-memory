# Jake Chapman — Investment Theses

## Direct venture investment

- **Explicit, current defense thesis:** Back early-stage companies developing emerging capabilities for U.S. and allied national security, with a clear path to defense application; Marque reports flexibility to lead or follow from Seed through Series A. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0138] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0139] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0140] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0141]

- **Explicit, defense-first rather than dual-use-only:** Chapman argues that waiting for commercial success can postpone defense-market fit by five to ten years and that a dual-use-only mandate misses important defense-first deep technologies. This does not exclude dual-use companies; sources also describe Marque as investing in defense, dual-use, and strategic technologies. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0017] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0026] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0017]

- **Explicit, forward-demand thesis:** Venture should predict future needs and invest ahead of them; in defense, multiyear legislative and acquisition signals can reveal where spending is headed. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0001] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0002] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0003]

- **Explicit, industrial-base transition:** Budgetary and strategic pressure will shift defense spending from incumbents toward startups, while crises create capital and political room for new suppliers. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0019] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0020]

- **Explicit, specialist-alpha thesis:** Defense clearances create institutional information asymmetry, and expertise navigating imperfect procurement can produce alpha; Chapman acknowledges that reforms gradually reduce this edge. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0116] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0117]

- **Explicit sector preferences:** Areas of interest include aerospace, space, flight, drones and connecting systems; contested communications and logistics; positioning, navigation, and timing in GPS-denied environments; advanced manufacturing; directed energy; energy; AI; autonomy; quantum computing; synthetic biology; climate technology; and ordinary enabling software or devices with defense uses. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0025] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0079] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0080] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0119]

- **Explicit saturation and category boundaries:** He described small unmanned aerial systems as oversupplied and said he does not invest in purely consumer-facing businesses, e-commerce, game studios, or adtech; novel technology or an original technological application is generally expected. These statements are source-date-specific and do not establish an eternal ban on every adjacent model. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0025] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0120] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0121]

- **Explicit mission-plus-returns thesis:** He wants consequential technology capable of materially advancing prosperity and is most excited when meaningful impact combines with very large financial upside. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0081] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0082] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0084] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0086] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0088] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0089] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0090]

- **Inferred founder thesis:** Persistent, mission-oriented founders who show genuine enthusiasm, answer hard questions thoughtfully, acknowledge uncertainty, and follow through are especially attractive. Operating advice suggests additional preference for defense teams with military or acquisition experience and early end-user contact, but those points should not be treated as universal prerequisites. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0005] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0006] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0009] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0011] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0012] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0048]

- **Explicit traction thesis:** Grants, one contract, and verbal interest do not demonstrate repeatable defense success; stronger evidence includes real adoption, budget and contracting pathways, programs of record, and pilot conversion to commercial deployment. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0023] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0029] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0108] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0187]

- **Explicit pricing thesis:** Company quality cannot be separated from entry price. Hot defense valuations may outrun realizable growth, and at Series A or later social-signal following can become uneconomic because of competing capital, pro-rata rights, and ownership targets. ## Fund allocation and portfolio construction

- **Explicit diversification thesis:** Although venture is individually risky, Chapman argues its low correlation can reduce risk in a broader portfolio; he separately describes defense as counter-cyclical and deserving an allocation in diversified portfolios. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0014] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0018]

- **Historical Alpha Bridge construction:** The fund paired deep tech with consumer because Chapman viewed their exit and risk-return profiles as complementary and aligned with GP interests. That consumer exposure differs from an exclusion of purely consumer-facing companies in an undated Signal profile identifying him with Alpha Bridge. The sources leave the timing and vehicle scope unresolved; they do not establish a later transition to Marque’s mandate. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0093] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0120]

- **Historical small-fund economics:** Chapman said modest ownership could drive fund returns: roughly 1% at a $1 billion-plus exit could return a tiny fund multiple times, while several moderate exits or one unicorn could support a 3x-net outcome. These are historical Alpha Bridge economics, not confirmed Marque targets. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0098] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0099]

- **AVCC mandate:** In 2022 Chapman described AVCC as a financially driven defense-technology fund using a hybrid for-profit/nonprofit structure and both public and private capital. Its scope extended from advanced deep technology to mundane enabling products with defense value. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0078] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0079] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0080] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0132] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0133]

## Operating-company strategy

- **Inferred, customer discovery:** Identify the end user, obtain feedback early, state who buys and why, and use small tests to estimate demand and acquisition cost. These are operating recommendations and strong diligence signals, not universal investment gates. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0006] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0065] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0066] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0106]

- **Inferred, execution discipline:** Favor completed work over promised work, dated and granular roadmaps over aspirations, and non-dilutive projects only when aligned with the existing roadmap and available capacity. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0027] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0028] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0053] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0054]

- **Explicit, defense go-to-market:** Work from day zero across user demand, requirements, priorities, budgets, contracts, and programs of record so procurement is ready when the product can scale. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0029]

- **Explicit, economic model:** Future venture-built defense primes may be more nimble than incumbents and avoid cost-plus contracting to deliver more capability at lower customer cost with higher margins. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0030]

- **Inferred, governance and support:** Preserve founder leadership except in extreme cases, recognize when zero-to-one skills cease to fit scaling needs, and favor engaged advisors whose equity is tied to defined contributions rather than prestigious names.
