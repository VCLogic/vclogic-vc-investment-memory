## Mandate and operating constraints

- **Firm vehicle, not personal balance sheet:** nearly all supported investment activity is attributed to 11 Tribes or its funds. The evidence does not establish a separate personal portfolio, public-markets book, real-estate allocation, majority acquisition strategy, or direct family-office vehicle owned by Phillips. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0187]

- **Stage and traction:** seed/Series A, increasingly post-revenue, normally around $500,000–$2 million of revenue, though older descriptions reached $3 million and exceptional signed contracts can offset pre-revenue risk. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0209] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0129] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0168]

- **Geography:** principally North America and non-coastal tier-two/tier-three ecosystems; early Fund I reporting included one Mexico investment. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0122] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0025]

- **Sector:** generalist, with historically reported concentrations in future of work, healthcare, data analytics, cybersecurity, food/agtech, financial services, education, CPG, and hardware; later sourcing emphasizes vertical AI and niche operator-led companies. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0074] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0130] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0192]

- **Exclusions and portfolio limits:** vice businesses, AI wrappers, and foundational LLM companies are express exclusions; existing sector concentration may block another otherwise suitable investment. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0032] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0131] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0102]

- **Capital and pricing:** Fund II statements point to $1–1.5 million checks, $2–3 million rounds, and source-dependent valuation ranges from roughly $10–15 million pre-money to $15–25 million post-money. These evolved from smaller Fund I checks and lower valuation targets and should not be collapsed into a timeless rule. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0127] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0128] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0160] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0065]

- **Ownership and control:** the firm historically described itself as a minority investor targeting about 5–7% plus pro rata rights; later strategy shifted toward leading rounds and taking board seats. No evidence supports a majority-ownership or acquisition mandate. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0104] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0310]

- **Decision governance:** an early four-person committee required unanimity; a later account names three investment-committee partners, suggesting process evolution or differing membership descriptions, with exact timing unresolved. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0373] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0152]

- **Founder alignment:** the firm’s late-diligence handshake agreement tests willingness to pursue durable, profitable growth and founder resilience rather than a heavily financed unicorn path. A mismatch can end the deal. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0126] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0137] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0172]

- **Source limitation:** portfolio counts, check sizes, valuation bands, leadership posture, and fund status changed across Fund I, Fund II, and later first-party articles. Aggregator profiles sometimes conflict with primary statements—for example, VC Sheet says the fund does not lead, while Phillips’s later first-party account says it leads virtually every round; the first-party dated strategy receives greater weight. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0307] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0310]

Phillips also reported an expectation that a large majority of investments would be qualifying stock held for five years. This is a source-reported holding orientation, not verification of any company’s tax status or a current legal conclusion. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0278]
