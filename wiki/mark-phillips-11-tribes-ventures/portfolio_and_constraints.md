## Mandate and operating constraints

- **Stage:** 11 Tribes is repeatedly described as an early-stage venture firm investing in technology companies; the record does not provide a complete stage policy beyond examples and profile descriptions. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0013] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0048]

- **Sector:** An outside profile lists SaaS, fintech, health, consumer, and enterprise, while Phillips acknowledges that some industries fall outside the mandate. Because no first-party source provides a complete list, current fit requires confirmation. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0108] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0036]

- **Geography:** VC Sheet reports niche, non-coastal markets including Chattanooga, Nashville, and Indianapolis, and The Org describes Phillips as Chicago-based. These are source-reported orientations, not proven geographic exclusion rules. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0107] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0095]

- **Check size:** Signal reports $100,000–$300,000 with a $200,000 sweet spot; The Pitch reports $5,000–$250,000 and includes show investments. Their conflicting scopes preclude a definitive institutional check-size rule. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0067] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0086]

- **Portfolio size:** Phillips states 20–25 companies, while a March 2024 first-party update reported 33 Fund I portfolio companies. Treat the former as stated architecture and the latter as reported historical exposure, with the discrepancy unresolved. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0042] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0078]

- **Reserve and follow-on behavior:** NQS received two additional tranches after the initial seed investment, demonstrating follow-on capacity in at least one case, but the evidence does not establish a general reserve ratio. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0006]

- **Leadership and governance:** Phillips says the firm leads virtually every round and seeks board influence, but the NQS seed was led by Long Run Capital and VC Sheet says the fund does not lead. These may reflect evolution, exceptions, or source error; confirm round by round. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0046] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0003] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0109]

- **Capital model:** The strategy favors capital-efficient companies, properly sized financing, profitable growth, founder ownership retention, and limited dependence on serial fundraising. These are preferences and underwriting considerations, not proof that every portfolio company satisfies each one. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0044] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0045] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0055] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0081]

- **Outcome model:** Moderate exits are acceptable and sometimes preferred; outside descriptions report comfort with approximately $75–250 million or $80–250 million outcomes, while Phillips’s writing discusses approximately $100 million and $100–250 million outcomes. These are illustrations, not hard minimums or maximums. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0041] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0049] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0050] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0074] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0107]

- **Valuation:** The firm avoids inflated rounds and underwrites to value; the clearest negative example is a pre-revenue AI company at a $50 million valuation. No general valuation ceiling is supplied. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0009] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0078]

- **Founder-resilience allocation:** The firm says 2% of each invested dollar is reserved for coaching, counseling, organizational health, and related support; third parties describe the allocation as non-dilutive. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0040] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0106] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0129]

## Fund-level chronology and source limitations

Fund I was reported at $11 million, with approximately 80–82 high-net-worth LPs depending on source wording; Fundingstack supplies the absolute close date of December 31, 2021. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0076] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0125] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0126]

Fund II reporting evolves rather than necessarily conflicts: more than $20 million raised in a January 4, 2024 letter, $23.5 million committed on March 4, more than $33 million in a Q2 update, $36 million in a Q3 update, and $46.4 million closed by the April 21, 2025 update. A later podcast description rounds this to $46 million from 220 high-net-worth investors and family offices. No transaction date has been derived from relative wording. Where only an article or episode date exists, the table reports the source date and leaves the underlying closing date unclear.
